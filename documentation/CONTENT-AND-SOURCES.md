# Content and sources

## The frame
- **Across:** the world around Singapore, from open and connected to fragmented and contested.
- **Down:** the breadth of the social compact, meaning how widely gains, risks and say are shared among citizens (income against GDP, who owns the home and wealth, how much of the budget and rules citizens can shape).
- Why these two: a city-state lives or dies on whether the world still needs the hub (outside its control) and whether its people stay with it while it adapts (inside its control).
- Common to all four: sea and heat; the silver generation; the 2061 water clock; AI and work. Pakistan 2047 used the same device (a front page for each world, "where we need your input").

## The four futures (2065)
A **Open Commons**: citizens' assembly, citizen dividend, care guarantee, co-owned water and power compact with Johor. B **Lifeboat Republic**: a smaller, fair, rationed economy; National Resilience Service. C **Gilded Hub**: record capital and compute, citizens become tenants as flat leases expire. D **Garrison City**: indoor parade, emergency order in year 14, care circles tolerated because they are useful.
