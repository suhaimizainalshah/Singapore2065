# Privacy notice (draft for the data-protection team to edit)

**Who runs this:** [organisation and contact to be confirmed].

**What stays on your phone.** Your photo, voice and emoji are used to make a picture or video on your own device. They are not uploaded. We do not see them.

**What we count, if you tap Allow.** Anonymous taps and choices, for example that someone spun, which future came up, which front-page sections were opened, whether a picture was shared, and your two feedback answers. We do not collect names, email addresses, photos, voice, typed text or which emoji you chose.

**How.** We use Google Analytics 4. It sets cookies (`_ga` and `_ga_` followed by the property code) that let it count visits without knowing who you are. Google signals and advertising features are off. Google may infer your country and device type.

**Small notes kept on your device.** Your choice about statistics, language, sound, vibration and motion are saved in your browser. If you arrive through a tagged link, a broad group label (for example "west") is kept for the tab only and sent with the anonymous counts. Nothing here identifies you.

**If you tap "No thanks".** Nothing is sent to Google, and the Google script is not loaded. The game works the same. If your browser sends Do Not Track, we treat that as no.

**Changing your mind.** Open About > Privacy at any time and switch statistics off. Clear the site's data in your browser to remove the cookies.

**How long.** [state the retention period set in Google Analytics, for example 14 months].

**Your rights and contact.** [access, deletion, objection and contact details as required in your country].

**Lawful basis.** Consent. [to be confirmed by the data-protection team]
